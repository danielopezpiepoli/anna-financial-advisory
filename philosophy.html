Core Principles & Ethics

The foundations of independent fiduciary counsel.

In a financial industry saturated with product pushing and opaque retrocessions, true advisory requires absolute structural independence.

01

Radical Independence & Fee-Only Model

I do not accept kickbacks, distributor fees, or commissions from banks, fund managers, or insurance brokers. The only entity compensating my counsel is you. This completely eliminates conflicts of interest and ensures our goals remain 100% aligned.

02

Uncompromising Discretion & Direct Dialogue

Private matters demand complete confidentiality. You work directly with me—not through anonymous associate layers or rotating relationship managers. All data and communications are treated with strict European privacy and professional discretion.

03

Cultural & Jurisdictional Fluency

Cross-border advisory requires more than legal compliance; it demands empathy for the human journey between European systems. I provide a natural, multilingual bridge between German regulatory expectations and international backgrounds.

04

Capital Preservation Over Speculative Hype

Building wealth takes decades; losing it through reckless structures can take weeks. My advisory philosophy prioritizes stability, generational continuity, multi-currency liquidity, and resilient structuring over volatile short-term trends.
