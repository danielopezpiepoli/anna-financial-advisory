Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our engagement structure, fee-only compensation, cross-border scope, and confidentiality standards.

Advisory Model & Fees

What does "Fee-Only" advisory mean, and why does it matter?

"Fee-only" means my compensation comes exclusively from direct client fees (hourly rates, fixed project pricing, or monthly retainers). I accept zero commissions, referral fees, or kickbacks from banks, fund managers, or insurance products. This guarantees that every strategy recommended is 100% focused on your interests without hidden agendas.

How does the initial discovery consultation work?

Our initial discovery conversation is a private, confidential session to understand your current cross-border circumstances, key challenges, and primary objectives. It is non-binding and allows both parties to assess whether an advisory mandate is mutually aligned.

Cross-Border & Relocation

Do you handle legal representation or tax filings directly?

Anna Advisory provides independent strategic financial counsel, asset coordination, and overarching roadmap architecture. For statutory court representation and formal tax return submissions in Germany, I work in synchronized collaboration with licensed German attorneys (Rechtsanwälte) and tax advisors (Steuerberater).

Which regional markets and jurisdictions do you specialize in?

My primary focus centers on the intersection between Germany/Western Europe and Eastern European cross-border dynamics (particularly Ukraine). I advise international founders, private families, and professionals establishing financial continuity across borders.

Confidentiality & Privacy

How is client data and financial confidentiality protected?

All communications and financial records are held under strict non-disclosure terms and full compliance with European GDPR (DSGVO) standards. Client interactions are direct with Anna, ensuring zero delegation to unauthorized third parties or commercial marketing systems.

Can consultations be conducted in languages other than German?

Yes. Consultations, documentation reviews, and strategic roadmaps are conducted fluently in English, German, Ukrainian, and Russian.
